Forecast Channel — native execution (recompiled PowerPC → WebAssembly)

idle — press boot to execute the channel binary

This page executes the real Forecast Channel executable (0000000d.app, decrypted from the WAD and statically recompiled to WebAssembly with DolRecomp — 381,400 PowerPC instructions, 94 generated C chunks).

Verified live behavior: boots through the channel's own init, enables FPU/GQRs via its real SPR sequence, runs its AX audio engine (DSP mailbox traffic on 0xCD0068xx), paces itself with mftb timers, and polls its WC24 data subsystem in the same loop it uses on hardware.

In progress: IOS IPC emulation (network stack), cooperative thread scheduling, and the GX→WebGL render bridge. The console log shows the channel's hardware traffic in real time; FC_DEBUG.dispatch() dumps the executed-block histogram and FC_DEBUG.trace() shows the control-flow graph.
