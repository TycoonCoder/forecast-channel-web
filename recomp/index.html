This page executes the real channel executable (0000000d.app, decrypted from the WAD and statically recompiled to WebAssembly with DolRecomp). HLE stubs stand in for Wii hardware. Rendering/audio bridges are staged: the current build runs the boot path and reports progress; GX→WebGL and AX→WebAudio bridges land next.
